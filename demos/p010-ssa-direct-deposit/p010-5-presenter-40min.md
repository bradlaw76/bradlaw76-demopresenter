**Presenter rule:** Daniel's voice components remain in Daniel's own environment. Brad opens Elena's separate portal and Dynamics service workflow, then Contact **before** the existing Case. The Contact banking alert is a proposed **read-only display derived from the related bank row**, not a second Contact Boolean and not yet deployed.

## Navigation budget

1. Daniel concludes his separate voice example; Brad does not reopen Daniel's voice panel.
2. Brad opens Elena's portal, points to the review alert and asks the chatbot for human help.
3. Brad switches to Dynamics Active Conversation **only if a real chat arrives**, otherwise a clearly prepared representative view.
4. Brad opens the exact Case-linked Elena Contact once for profile, benefits, Super Six and Timeline. The desired banking alert is not yet an installed Contact component.
5. Brad opens the enhanced Elena Case once for Case Activity Center, SharePoint/TED, three incomplete checklist responses, masked banking and H089 Review & Resolution. H087 SSA Agent Workbench is published but not embedded; preserve the current earnings/fraud panel.
6. Brad shows three H071 reports, then Beth shows one read-only administration view.

Don't create a duplicate Case for the presentation or claim the protected bank update/flag clearance has succeeded.

**Default safe banking path:** Leave the actual record and Case pending. Describe the sequence and show a distinctly labeled before/after illustration only. A presenter guide never approves or performs a Dataverse write or financial record operation.

## Presenter transitions

**Daniel → Brad:** “That concludes Sofía's independent voice example in my environment. Brad will now open Elena's different direct-deposit issue through the portal.”

**Portal → representative:** “The assistant can explain the issue, but protected banking changes require a human. Let's see the representative view.”

**Conversation → Contact:** “Now we know why Elena needs assistance. Let's review her constituent context and available history.”

**Contact → Case:** “We've established Elena's profile and verification gaps. Now we'll manage the actual service work in her Case.”

**Case → reporting:** “The request is correctly not ready for closure. Let's review the workload and supervisor perspectives.”

**Brad → Beth:** “We've seen the customer, representative and Case experience. Beth will close with administration and governance.”

**Beth → executive:** “That completes the governed service story and the remaining pilot acceptance decisions.”
